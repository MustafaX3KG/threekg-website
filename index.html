ThreeKG is an independent design practice working with founders and cultural brands who'd rather show than explain. We design identities, websites and the small details in between — with a taste for the quiet confidence that makes work memorable.

We take on a handful of projects at a time so the people you brief are the people doing the work. Every project starts with listening, ends with launch, and hopefully starts a conversation that lasts longer than the invoices.
